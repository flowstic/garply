Subscriptions & Pro Access

  • Restoring Purchases: In the app, open the Profile Menu (tap your avatar) and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Manage or Cancel: Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple or Google:
    • iOS: Apple Subscriptions Manager
    • Android: Google Play Subscriptions
  • Refunds: Since billing is processed through Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing, refund requests must be submitted through their respective store support portals.

Account & Data Management

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated watchlists/preferences:

  • In the app: Tap your profile avatar > Delete Account.
  • Or email us at flowstic.io@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is the GARP Score? It is a quantitative 0–100 rating balancing forward PEG ratio, 3-year EPS growth compound rate, forward P/E, debt-to-equity leverage, and cash flow generation.
  • How often is stock data refreshed? Fundamental multiples and market prices are updated once daily overnight around 2:00 AM UTC (shortly after US market after-hours close), ensuring all metrics and screens are fully finalized well before the next trading day.
